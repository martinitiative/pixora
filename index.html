FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is PIXORA really free?
Yes — the Free plan is free forever. Upgrade to Pro anytime for unlimited storage.
How do I pay?
Manual payment via Nagad, Bkash or Rocket. Choose a plan, send the money, then submit your TrxID.
How long does verification take?
Usually within a few hours. Track status in "My Orders".
What formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP — up to 32MB on Free.
Can I delete my images?
Absolutely. You own your images.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Encrypted, secure, and never sold.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy is a core value at PIXORA. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, hashed password.
  • Content: images, titles, descriptions.
  • Usage data: browser, IP, patterns.
  • Payment data: TrxIDs you submit.

2. How We Use Your Data

To operate PIXORA, authenticate you, deliver your images, verify payments and provide support. We never sell your data.

3. Payment Information

We do not store full payment credentials. We only store TrxIDs for verification.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in.

5. Your Rights

Access, correct, export or delete your data anytime. Email privacy@pixora.app.

6. Security

HTTPS, encrypted storage, best practices.
